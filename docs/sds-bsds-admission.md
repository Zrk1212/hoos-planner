# UVA School of Data Science — B.S. in Data Science (BSDS): admission + degree requirements

Research date: 2026-10-19. Audience: a UVA first-year (entered fall 2026, College of Arts & Sciences) planning spring 2027 courses and a possible BSDS and/or McIntire application.

Primary sources used: datascience.virginia.edu (Admissions, FAQ, Academics, Assured Enrollment, Deadlines pages, and news posts), the UVA Undergraduate Record 2026-2027 (records.ureg.virginia.edu, the official repository of requirements), The Cavalier Daily, theCourseForum. Reddit r/UVA threads: none surfaced in any search (see "Gaps" at the end). Facts I could not confirm are marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## 1. How current UVA students get in

**Path:** Internal application in the **spring of first year only**; admitted students transfer into the School of Data Science (SDS) the summer after first year and start the three-year major in second year. The Record is explicit: "only students at the University of Virginia who have just completed their first year may apply... Students in their second, third, or fourth year at the University of Virginia may not apply. Students transferring from colleges or universities outside of the University of Virginia may not apply." Students from any UVA school may apply (most come from the College; Engineering students also apply).
- Record 2026-27, SDS Academic Rules: https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/content.php?catoid=72&navoid=6653
- SDS Admissions page: https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/admissions
- SDS FAQ: https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/faq

There are now **two routes** for the fall 2027 entering cohort (i.e., for students who entered UVA in fall 2026):

### Route A — Assured Enrollment (new pilot, 2026-27 academic year, non-binding)
- Intent to Enroll form opens Thu Sept 3, 2026; **deadline Sun Nov 1, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET**.
- Decisions released Mon **Nov 9, 2026** (the Admissions page's timeline block says Nov 6 — the Deadlines page and Assured Enrollment page say Nov 9; treat Nov 9 as current).
- Deadline to respond to the conditional offer: Fri **Nov 20, 2026** (the Admissions page's prose says Dec 1 in one spot; the Deadlines page says Nov 20 — plan for Nov 20).
- Condition: complete the published prerequisites by the end of first year (24 UVA credit hours including DS 1001 with C- or better, plus the Programming Requirement) and maintain satisfactory academic progress; students transfer into SDS in summer 2027.
- Benefits: a reserved seat without the competitive application, priority registration for fall 2027 BSDS courses, BSDS advising, event invitations. Not binding — you can still choose another major.
- Sources: https://datascience.virginia.edu/bsds/assured-enrollment ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/deadlines-and-events ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/news/assured-enrollment-pilot-gives-first-year-students-early-pathway-bs-data-science (July 22, 2026) ; Cavalier Daily, Sept 3, 2026: https://www.cavalierdaily.com/article/2026/09/uva-school-of-data-science-announces-first-of-its-kind-assured-enrollment-program
- Whether Assured Enrollment seats are capped: **UNVERIFIED** (no source states a cap).

### Route B — Regular application (fall 2027 entry)
- Application opens **Wed Jan 27, 2027**; **deadline Tue May 4, 2027, 12:00 p.m. ET**; decisions released **Fri June 11, 2027**; respond by **Fri June 18, 2027**; waitlist closes **Sun Aug 1, 2027**. (FAQ adds: committee reviews after spring grades post; waitlist is unranked, released "throughout July" space permitting; no supplemental materials or letters of continued interest accepted.)
- Sources: https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/deadlines-and-events ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/admissions ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/faq
- Apply portal: https://apply.internal.virginia.edu/apply/

### How many admitted / acceptance rate
- No acceptance rate is published anywhere I could find. Cohort sizes: inaugural cohort (entered fall 2024, Class of 2027) = **75** students; fall 2026 cohort (Class of 2029) = **107** students (71 Virginians, 36 non-Virginians, 13 international). Cavalier Daily quotes "roughly 100-125 students per class in the major." The Record cites "the limited number of seats."

## 2. Prerequisites (exact)

Per the Undergraduate Record 2026-27 (official) — https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=72&poid=11828 and the SDS Academic Rules page:

1. **24 credit hours completed at UVA by the end of spring of first year** ("may include general education credits, credits toward another major or minor, and electives").
2. **DS 1001 – Foundation of Data Science (3 credits)** — "Must be taken at UVA and completed with a grade of C- or higher. This may not be taken on a Credit/No Credit basis." Record course entry: "Requisites: Must be a 1st Year student to enroll." https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=72&coid=484224
3. **Programming Requirement**, met by ONE of:
   - **DS 1002 – Programming for Data Science (3 credits)** — Record note: "Students can't enroll if previously taken any of the following courses: CS 1110, CS 1111, CS 1112, CS 1113, CS 1120, PHYS 1655." https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=72&coid=484003
   - **CS 1110 / CS 1111 / CS 1112 / CS 1113 – Introduction to Programming (3 credits each)**
   - **PHYS 1655 – Introduction to Python for Scientists and Engineers (3 credits)**
   - **Test credit: AP Computer Science A score 4 or 5; IB HL Computer Science 5, 6, or 7**
   - (The CS 1110 place-out test was accepted through 2025-26 but "Effective Fall 2026, this will no longer be an option" — Record 2025-26: https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=67&poid=10260)

**In-progress rule:** the Admissions page and FAQ say DS 1001 and DS 1002 "must be completed or in progress at the time of application" (application is due May 4, before spring grades). Both must be taken for a letter grade — "CR/NC will not meet the prerequisite." Both are offered fall and spring, have no prerequisites, can be taken in any order, and **may be taken concurrently**. https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/admissions ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/faq

**Minimum grade:** C- or higher in DS 1001 (Record). No published minimum grade for the programming course beyond needing a letter grade (Record says "Enrollment in" one of the listed courses); **UNVERIFIED** whether a D in CS 1110/DS 1002 would be accepted.

**Math:** Calculus I is **NOT required for admission** but strongly recommended in first year; it IS a degree requirement (see §4) and must be done before DS 2126/DS 3025 Mathematics for Data Science. Options: MATH 1190, MATH 1210, MATH 1310 (or MATH 1191+1192 sequence), APMA 1090 (Engineering transfers), or higher calculus (MATH 1220, 1320, 2310, 2315). **AP Calc AB or BC score 4 or 5, or IB HL Math 5-7, satisfies the math requirement.** The Record adds: "The Math Requirement cannot satisfy the Quantification, Computation, and Data Analysis General Education Requirement."
- Sources: Record 2026-27 program page; FAQ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/news/bs-data-science-uva-what-prospective-students-need-know (Apr 1, 2026)

**Statistics:** No statistics course (STAT 1601, STAT 2120, DS 2003, etc.) is a prerequisite or a BSDS degree requirement. Probability/inference are taught inside the major (DS 2026, DS 3026). The FAQ and Record do not mention any STAT course.

**AP credit summary:** AP CS A (4/5) satisfies the Programming Requirement. AP Calc AB/BC (4/5) satisfies the degree math requirement. AP Statistics: no role in BSDS prerequisites or requirements (**UNVERIFIED** only in the sense that no page mentions it at all). Nothing substitutes for DS 1001 — it must be taken at UVA.

## 3. What is evaluated

- Application contents (Admissions page, 2026-27 cycle): the online application with SIS credentials (pulls your UVA transcript automatically) plus **two required short-answer questions and one optional one, each ≤250 words**: (1) how your background/experiences will help you contribute to a collaborative community; (2) a data-related experience that deepened your interest; (3) optional context (grade anomalies, gaps). https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/admissions
- Earlier cycles also required a one-page resume and one recommendation (not from SDS faculty) — the April 2026 news post still lists those; the current Admissions page lists only the short answers. Treat resume/recommendation as **UNVERIFIED for the 2027 cycle** and check the live application in January.
- **No interview. No test scores or supplemental materials. Holistic review; demonstrated interest is not considered.** (FAQ)
- **No minimum GPA and no published average GPA.** SDS admissions blog (Jan 9, 2025): GPA is one factor in a holistic review; they value challenging yourself across disciplines; prior DS experience not required. https://datascience.virginia.edu/news/inside-uva-school-data-science-what-makes-strong-bsds-applicant
- Decisions: Admit / Waitlist / Deny; applying to McIntire at the same time does not affect review (FAQ).
- Average GPA of admits: **UNVERIFIED / not published**.

## 4. Full BSDS degree requirements (Undergraduate Record 2026-2027)

Source: https://records.ureg.virginia.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=72&poid=11828 (cross-checked with https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/academics)

**Totals:** 120 credits; 49 DS credits; 60 credits must be taken at UVA; minimum cumulative GPA 2.00. Three-year residency in SDS; degree within eight semesters of matriculation. Core courses must be taken for a letter grade with D or better; no withdrawals from core courses; no substitutions.

**Core curriculum (40 credits):**
- DS 2022 Introduction to Computing (3) — Record: "Must be a declared Data Science major"
- DS 2023 Communicating with Data (3)
- DS 2024 Ethics & Policy in Data Science (3)
- DS 2026 Computational Probability (3)
- DS 2126 Mathematics for Data Science (4) (the SDS Academics page shows this as DS 3025 in year-1 spring; the 2026-27 Record lists DS 2126)
- DS 3021 Predictive Analytics (3)
- DS 3022 Data Engineering (3)
- DS 3023 Data by Design (3)
- DS 3026 Principles of Inference and Prediction (3)
- DS 4021 Machine Learning (3)
- DS 4024 Explainable AI (3)
- DS 4022 Data Science Project (3) — capstone; also satisfies the Second Writing Requirement
- Plus ONE of MATH 1190 (4) / MATH 1210 (3) / MATH 1310 (4), or AP/IB/transfer/higher-calculus credit as above.

**Sequencing (SDS Academics page):** Year 1 of major (2nd year at UVA): fall DS 2022, DS 2023, DS 2026 + Calc I if not done; spring DS 2024, DS 3021, DS 2126. Year 2: fall DS 3022, DS 4021 (study abroad option: Valencia, Copenhagen/DIS, Stockholm); spring DS 3026, DS 3023, DS 4024 + one elective. Year 3: fall two electives; spring DS 4022 + optional elective. https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/academics

**Data Science electives (9 credits):** three upper-level DS courses, optionally as a concentration (Analytics: DS 4121/4125/4126/5001/5080/5122/5062; Systems: DS 4220/4221/5220/5221; Design: DS 4023/4329/5320; Value: DS 4422/4423/5420/5320) or any 3 from those lists. Collaborative concentrations (optional, may take 1+): Educational Analytics, Neuroscience, **Analytical Accounting (CGBM 2110, CGBM 2710, COMM 3110, COMM 3559 — McIntire courses)**, Environmental Science, Astronomy, Human Movement & Physiology, Mathematics, Economics, Global Public Health, Public Policy & Leadership. Only 3 credits of DS 4993/4995 independent study count.

**General education (33 credits) — SDS has its OWN gen-ed, not the College's:** "The School of Data Science will accept students from any of the College of Arts & Sciences general education curricular programs (Echols Scholars Program, Engagements Pathway, Disciplines Plus Pathway or Forums curriculum) and any other school of enrollment at UVA. Search course attributes in SIS for a list of courses that count toward general education requirements. The School of Data Science waives all general education curricular requirements for Echols Scholars." The FAQ confirms BSDS students "would not need to complete the general education requirements of the College."

SDS gen-ed categories (they mirror the College's Literacies + Disciplines, with NO Engagements and NO world-language requirement):
- Rhetoric for the 21st Century: 6 credits = First Writing Requirement (3; ENWR, or ENGR 1010+1020 for E-School transfers) + Second Writing (3) — **DS 4022 satisfies the Second Writing Requirement**
- Quantification, Computation, and Data Analysis: 6 credits — **DS 2026 satisfies 3 credits**; the calculus requirement may NOT count toward this
- Artistic, Interpretive, and Philosophical Inquiry: 3
- Chemical, Mathematical, and Physical Universe: 3
- Cultures and Societies of the World: 3
- Historical Perspectives: 3
- Living Systems: 3
- Social and Economic Systems: 3
- Science and Society: 3 — **DS 1001 satisfies this**
(Double-counting governed by the Record's "Simultaneous Counting of Courses" policy.)

**Do first-year College courses carry over?** Yes. The 24-credit prerequisite explicitly counts gen-ed, other-major and elective credits; the Academics page says any first-year course not counting toward SDS gen-ed still counts toward the 120-credit total; SDS advises first-years to "stay on track with the curricular requirements of their home school" and to finish First Writing + Calc I in first year. Note Engagements (EGMT) courses have no SDS gen-ed category, so they become general electives toward 120. The FAQ adds: AP/IB/dual-enrollment credit cannot shorten the program below three years but gives flexibility/lighter loads. Echols Scholars keep their gen-ed waiver but still need Calc I and lose Echols priority registration. Minimum 12 credits/semester, 15 recommended, max 18 without petition.
- Sources: Record program page; https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/academics ; FAQ.

## 5. DS 1002 vs CS 1110 for spring 2027

**Equivalence per SDS:** The Record lists DS 1002, CS 1110, CS 1111, CS 1112, CS 1113 and PHYS 1655 as fully interchangeable ways to meet the Programming Requirement; the FAQ frames CS 1110/1111/1112 as what to take "in lieu of DS 1002." Admission review does not distinguish them. **They are mutually exclusive:** DS 1002's Record entry bars enrollment if you have taken CS 1110/1111/1112/1113/1120 or PHYS 1655, and the CS department allows credit for only one of CS 1110/1111/1112/1113/1120. So pick one.

**Credits:** DS 1002 = 3 credits; CS 1110 = 3 credits (Record 2026-27 program page lists both at 3; CS advising page confirms 3 for CS 1110/1111/1112/1113). https://uvacsadvising.org/courses.html

**Content/format differences:**
- DS 1002: Python AND R (pandas, tidyverse), plus Git/GitHub; labs-over-exams, Jupyter notebooks; prepares directly for the DS 2022 systems sequence. Spring 2027 instructor per theCourseForum: Jianxin Xie, two MoWe sections (2:00-3:15, 3:30-4:45); historical avg GPA ≈3.92, difficulty 1.0/5. https://thecourseforum.com/course/DS/1002/ ; https://datascience.virginia.edu/news/what-can-you-expect-bsds-prerequistes-ds-1001-and-ds-1002
- CS 1110: Python only, lecture + required lab, exams; the standard CS-minor/CS-BA entry course. Spring 2027 per theCourseForum: Arohi Khargonkar, MWF 12:00 and 1:00 sections; avg GPA ≈3.40, difficulty 2.9/5. https://thecourseforum.com/course/CS/1110/ ; https://engineering.virginia.edu/department/computer-science/academics/undergraduate-programs/introduction-programming-and-place-out-tests

**Which is better for a McIntire-also applicant:** McIntire's first-year prerequisites are COMM 1800, ECON 2010 and the First Writing Requirement — no programming course — so neither DS 1002 nor CS 1110 affects McIntire eligibility. https://www.commerce.virginia.edu/bs-commerce/admissions/first-years . Practical trade-off: CS 1110 keeps the CS minor / Computer Science B.A. door open (DS 1002 does not count toward CS programs) and signals rigor to a GPA-conscious reviewer, but it is a harder course (lower average GPA) in a spring when GPA matters for both applications; DS 1002 is the easier, SDS-native option with R + Git exposure and is what SDS expects. If BSDS is the primary goal and McIntire the hedge, DS 1002 is the lower-risk choice; if you might end up in the College with a CS minor, CS 1110.

**College gen-ed credit for these courses (Literacies/Disciplines):** The College's QCD literacy requires two 3-4 credit courses that carry the "Quantification" attribute in SIS (search Course Attributes → "Quantification"); it is "not a Calculus requirement." https://gened.as.virginia.edu/quantification-computation-and-data-analysis . **Verified via live SIS class-search data (pulled 2026-10-19 by the gened-research thread from https://sisuva.admin.virginia.edu/psc/ihprd/UVSS/SA/s/WEBLIB_HCX_CM.H_CLASS_SEARCH.FieldFormula.IScript_ClassSearch?institution=UVA01&term=1272&subject=DS&page=1 , terms 1268 = fall 2026 and 1272 = spring 2027):** DS 1001, DS 1002, DS 2002/2003/2004, CS 1110, CS 1112 and CS 1113 carry **no** College QCD attribute (ASUQ-QCD) and **no** Disciplines attribute (ASUD-*). Courses that DO carry ASUQ-QCD include STAT 1601, STAT 2120, STAT 1100, MATH 1210/1220/1310/1320, PSYC 2005; ECON 2010 carries ASUD-SES (Social & Economic Systems) only. So for a College student neither DS 1002 nor CS 1110 helps with the College's QCD literacy or any Discipline — they are electives toward the College's 120 unless you enter SDS. The BSDS Record's note that DS 1001 satisfies "Science and Society" and DS 2026 satisfies QCD applies inside the SDS's own gen-ed, not the College's SIS coding.

## 6. BSDS + McIntire

- **Applying to both in the same spring is allowed** and the decisions are independent: "Yes, you may apply to both our B.S. in Data Science and the McIntire School of Commerce's B.S. in Commerce at the same time." Both applications have the same calendar: open Jan 27, 2027, due May 4, 2027, 12 p.m. ET. https://datascience.virginia.edu/degrees/bsds/faq ; https://www.commerce.virginia.edu/bs-commerce/admissions/first-years
- **Double major / dual degree NOT allowed:** "B.S. in Data [Science] Students may not double-major with the B.S. in Commerce" — if admitted to both "you would need to choose one." BSDS students may double-major only with a College of Arts & Sciences program, and may hold up to two minors from other schools (but not the DS minor). (FAQ)
- Commerce-side alternatives: a Commerce major can add the **Data Science minor** (SDS minor open to students in any school who have declared a major; 15 graded credits, DS 1001 can count) — Record Academic Rules page. The BSDS side offers an **Analytical Accounting collaborative concentration** using McIntire courses (CGBM 2110, CGBM 2710, COMM 3110, COMM 3559). Whether McIntire offers a formal data-science track/concentration inside the B.S. Commerce: **UNVERIFIED here** (covered by the McIntire research thread).
- Note the timing asymmetry: Assured Enrollment (Nov 1 intent, Nov 20 response) is non-binding, so a student can hold an Assured Enrollment seat and still apply to McIntire in spring; nothing on the SDS pages forbids that (**UNVERIFIED** that SDS has no policy against it — ask sdsadmissions@virginia.edu).

## Decision-relevant checklist for spring 2027 (student entered fall 2026, College)

1. If DS 1001 was not taken fall 2026: enroll in **DS 1001 (3 cr, letter grade, first-years only)** in spring 2027 — it is non-substitutable and mandatory for both routes.
2. Take **one** programming course: DS 1002 or CS 1110 (both 3 cr, letter grade). They are mutually exclusive.
3. Be at ≥24 UVA credits by end of spring 2027 (test credit does not count toward the 24) — typical 15+15 schedule clears this.
4. Finish the First Writing Requirement and Calculus I (MATH 1210/1310, or AP 4/5) in first year — not required to apply but required for the degree and strongly recommended by SDS.
5. Assured Enrollment intent form was due **Nov 1, 2026** — if the Nov 1 window is missed, the regular application (Jan 27 – May 4, 2027) remains.
6. For McIntire in parallel: COMM 1800 + ECON 2010 + First Writing in first year; same May 4, 2027 deadline; can apply to both, but cannot do both degrees.
